nordhaus_1967_table1.csv is transcribed by hand from Table 1, "Optimal Life of Patent for
Run-of-the-Mill Process Inventions", page 29 of William D. Nordhaus, "The Optimal Life of a
Patent," Cowles Foundation Discussion Paper No. 241, Yale University, November 27, 1967.
Rows are the demand elasticity d, columns the proportional cost reduction B, cells the optimal
patent life T in years, computed by Nordhaus with rho = 0.20 and sigma = 9. "n" marks cells he
classed as drastic inventions and left out of the table.
Source PDF: https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-08/d0241.pdf (accessed 2026-11-01).
